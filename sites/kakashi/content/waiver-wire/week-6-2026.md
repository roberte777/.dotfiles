Week 5 wasn't the injury avalanche everyone feared, but the damage that
landed rewrites four depth charts. **Philadelphia** lost the third runner:
**Will Shipley** suffered a partial plantar-fascia tear in London (Schefter,
out multiple weeks) behind **Saquon Barkley** (hamstring, week-to-week, a
"could return Week 6" maybe) and **Tank Bigsby** (core-muscle surgery, IR,
4-6 weeks) — leaving practice-squad call-up **Dameon Pierce**, who played 48
snaps Sunday and is the projected starter if Barkley can't go. **Pittsburgh**
is down to DK Metcalf and a rookie: **Michael Pittman Jr.** (plantar
fasciitis) hit IR for at least four games, **Roman Wilson** has a finger/hand
injury expected to cost "a couple of weeks" (Fowler), and **Ben Skowronek**
added a calf — which crowns second-round rookie **Germie Bernard**, who ran
94% of the routes and went 6-80 on nine targets Sunday. **Houston's David
Montgomery** (groin, week-to-week) ceded the workhorse job to **Woody Marks**
— except Marks is 53% rostered, technically off our sub-50% wire. Other
landmines: **CeeDee Lamb**'s quad bruise (Ryan Flournoy watch), **Deebo
Samuel**'s hamstring MRI on a Niners WR room already missing Pearsall (out
for the season), **A.J. Brown** (eligible, unlikely) and
**Mack Hollins** (IR) leaving **Efton Chism III** and **DeMario Douglas**
running routes in New England, **Quinshon Judkins** in a walking boot plus
**Raheim Sanders**' hip (Jaleel McLaughlin is Cleveland's only healthy RB),
**Michael Penix**'s ribs, **Tyler Shough**'s hand, **KC Concepcion**'s
head, **T.J. Hockenson** and **Kenyon Sadiq** concussions (both on bye),
and **Tre' Harris** joining an LAC receiver ward already without McConkey
and Johnston. The **Marvin Harrison Jr. trade to Houston** promoted
**Kendrick Bourne** to Arizona's WR2 and makes C.J. Stroud a real
streaming target. **Tyreek Hill** remains unsigned. Four teams idle —
Cincinnati, Detroit, Miami, Minnesota.

## Stashes & lottery tickets

- **Woody Marks, RB, HOU (53%)** — technically above our wire threshold, but every single source lists him as the No. 1 add if your league has him uncaptured (CBS: "at least 20% of your remaining FAAB"; NBC: "this is where to spend it"). Four straight 8.2+ PPR games and a workhorse share with Montgomery week-to-week. London draw at Jacksonville is the one bad word about Week 6 itself.
- **MarShawn Lloyd, RB, GB (49–60%)** — 44% snap share and two TDs (16.7 PPR) with Jacobs still on the commissioner's exempt list and Brooks on IR. Dallas is No. 8 in points allowed to RBs (CBS: flex, up to 10% FAAB). He's probably rostered in your league; if not, you're picking a starting fantasy RB off the 60% line.
- **Efton Chism III, WR, NE (1–3%)** — team-high six targets, second among Pats WRs in routes, and Fitz's whole pitch is "if you aren't too hung up on pedigree." NYJ then CHI are friendly draws. Zero-dollar add in deep leagues.
- **Jaleel McLaughlin, RB, CLE (0–1%)** — the last healthy Brown: Judkins in a boot (day-to-day per Monken), Sanders a hip, Sampson IR. Fitz says monitor and bid accordingly; CBS is skeptical they'd feature him even with Judkins out. That skepticism is why he costs a dollar, not ten.
- **Ryan Flournoy, WR, DAL (7–8%)** — the CeeDee Lamb quad-bruise insurance. If Lamb can't go (bruise, not tear, and a Thursday gave him extra rest — coin flip), Fitz says Flournoy "suddenly becomes a playable asset" after a 48-yard bomb on four targets vs the Bucs. Conditional buy, $1–2.
- **Dontayvion Wicks, WR, PHI (44%)** — Fitz's #3 add: 60+ yards in three of five games and eight targets last week, and a third straight DeVonta Smith absence makes him flex-worthy vs Carolina. Check Smith's practice reports before the bid; Darius Cooper (6–8%, 4 TDs in 4 games) is the TD-flavor alternative and Fitz's explicit Fool's Gold warning.
- **Tyler Higbee, TE, LAR (3–4%)** — with Terrance Ferguson (ankle) out until Week 8, Higbee is the Rams' TE1: 18-212-1 on 23 targets over three games, and Arizona has yielded 30 catches, 363 yards and FOUR TDs to tight ends (Fitz). Short-lived, which in this market is the point.
- **Zach Charbonnet, RB, SEA (62–84%)** — NFL.com's No. 1 priority overall and RotoBaller's 10+-team add, but he's rostered almost everywhere. The real message: Seattle plays at Denver Thursday, he won't play Week 6, and when he's healthy he's their best back — make sure he's *on* your roster, not waived by accident.
- **Tyreek Hill, WR, FA (32%)** — still unsigned, still Rosenhaus saying "soon," still the Chiefs as the fantasy-logical landing (RotoBaller: "can you afford to eat the roster spot to gamble on a Kansas City reunion?"). Xavier Worthy (58%) is the paired lever if Rashee Rice's hamstring lingers. A buck in dynasty; nothing in redraft unless free.
- **Jaylen Wright, RB, MIA (bye)** — Ollie Gordon left Week 5 hurt with an undisclosed issue and Miami idles this week. If Gordon's scan is ugly, Wright inherits a lead-back audition against a post-Henderson-ankle Patriots front. Free right now, mandatory next Tuesday.
- **James Conner, RB, ARI (6%)** — Boone's No. 10 pickup: sitting on IR in Arizona, "extreme long shot" but a live trade candidate before the deadline if the Cardinals move him. IR-stash in points-per-week formats.
- **The handcuff cellar** — Fitz, Boone and CBS all re-list the gang: Kaelon Black (26–33%, McCaffrey), Mike Washington Jr. (43–44%, a banged-up Jeanty), Emmett Johnson (22–24%, Walker's durability), Tyler Allgeier (45–47%, Love's "tired" comments), Brian Robinson Jr. (~50%), Sione Vaki (28%, Gibbs) and Tyler Goodson (14%, Dallas' clear RB2 vs Luepke per Dynasty Nerds).
- **Emergency QB bench** — Dynasty Nerds' dynasty FAAB board: Case Keenum (Bears — Caleb Williams "has a chance" to return Week 6 per Rapoport, and Keenum already took over from Bagent) and Tyler Huntley (Ravens, still starting with Lamar short of progress). Redraft desperation per Boone: Huntley, Jalon Daniels (4%), Deshaun Watson, Geno Smith, Cam Ward. **Fernando Mendoza** (5%) is the deep stash worth a dollar before the Cousins sunset.

## Drop candidates

- **Justin Herbert** (QB) — Boone, CBS (69% in-house) and Jahnke's chorus, now terminal: 15 or fewer fantasy points in every one of five games, and the McConkey-foot/Johnston-chest/Harris-hamstring receiver ward is being run by the Chargers' medical staff. Bye in Week 7. Cut.
- **Bo Nix** (QB) — Boone + Fitz + CBS: under 15 points in four of five, 188 yards per game, one multi-TD game in five starts, and now Seattle's defense (11 sacks, two picks of Darnold last Sunday) in Thursday's primetime slot.
- **Sam Darnold** (QB) — Boone's third QB cut: the Seahawks lost two picks to the 49ers and he's a below-average stream asset in a top-heavy market. Debatable given the Smith-Njigba/Kupp room, but nobody's bidding on him.
- **Will Shipley** (RB) — CBS says safely cuttable in most leagues: partial plantar-fascia tear, multiple weeks, with Barkley possibly back this Sunday. The wire cycle completes; he was last week's must-add.
- **Kenneth Gainwell** (RB) — Fitz + CBS: never more than five carries, 2.6 YPC, seven or fewer touches every game, and Sean Tucker — not him — gets the early-down relief work if Bucky Irving blinks.
- **Brian Thomas Jr.** (WR) — Boone + Fitz: 3-25-1 over three games, zero targets converted vs Philadelphia, sub-two-thirds snap share every week, and the Jags publicly won't trade him. The 2024 version is gone for this season.
- **Chris Rodriguez Jr.** (RB) — Fitz: no stand-alone value; Bhayshul Tuten ran 49 snaps to his nine in London. Jacksonville's committee is Tuten plus Strange.
- **David Montgomery** (RB) — disputed: Boone says cut (32 scrimmage yards a game since Week 1, groin), Fitz says hold (favorable post-bye schedule if it's minor). Middle path: you're already rostering Marks in most leagues where this matters; drop Monty only if you need the spot.
- **Rico Dowdle** (RB) and **Mike Gesicki** (TE) — Fitz's "droppable with a chance of regret": Warren has taken Pittsburgh's backfield, and Gesicki's TD-per-catch rate is a math anomaly on a bye-week team.
- Also on the lists: **Jake Ferguson** and **Dalton Schultz** (TE, Boone again — the two-TE nothing), **Chris Godwin** and **Michael Pittman Jr.** (WR — injured, but roster-spot dead weight in season-long formats), **Jonathon Brooks** and **Jadarian Price** (RB, Boone), and Fool's Gold **Roschon Johnson** (garbage-time stat line, per Fitz).
