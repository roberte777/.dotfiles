*Published Tue 29 Sep · rostered % = Yahoo · FAAB on a $100 budget ·
sources: FantasyPros (Fitzmaurice), Yahoo (Boone), PFF (Jahnke), RotoBaller
(Mariano), FanDuel Research, CBS (Cummings), Yahoo/NBC injury reports.
PlayerProfiler, Dynasty Nerdz and Yardbarker had nothing week-different this
run — the wire consensus is unusually one-source-shaped (injuries), so the
cross-checks below are mostly about pricing, not personnel.*

Week 3's skill-position injuries piled onto Week 2's QB
carnage — **Caleb Williams** (hamstring) and **Jayden Daniels** (elbow,
practicing this week; Mariota stays hot either way) haven't come back while **De'Von Achane**
(torn ACL, season over), **Breece Hall** (quad/thigh, week-to-week per
Rapoport), **Travis Etienne** (hamstring aggravation), **Justin Jefferson**
(ankle sprain, good imaging, game-time call), **Mike Evans** (rib strain,
day-to-day), **Jalen Coker** (quad), and — the bitter pill — **Jonah
Coleman**, sent to short-term IR with a high ankle sprain the Broncos kept
quiet until after waivers ran last week. On the QB side, **Baker Mayfield**
dislocated his throwing-hand thumb and Todd Bowles says at least three weeks,
throwing Tampa's whole passing game into the Jalon Daniels experiment.
FantasyPros graded this week's wire a **C-** — thin at receiver, bloated at
injury-driven backfield options. Bid accordingly.

## Stashes & handcuffs

- **Jaylen Wright, RB, MIA (2%)** — was doubtful/inactive with a stinger and foot. If he practices, the Miami backfield splits and Gordon's ceiling drops. Speculate cheaply.
- **Kendre Miller, RB, NO (1%)** — 14 snaps in Etienne's absence; if the hamstring costs Etienne multiple weeks, a buck bid could buy the early-down job away from a 31-year-old.
- **Mike Washington Jr., RB, LV (34%)** — 36-yard TD run in New Orleans; best handcuff insurance on a 3-0 Raiders team while Ashton Jeanty (ankle) is banged up.
- **Chris Bell, WR, MIA (6%)** — 4-67-0 on seven targets, a career day for a former first-round talent back from last year's ACL tear. The upside stash of a talent-starved offense. Caleb Douglas (22%, high ankle) is the companion stash if he returns to practice.
- **Tyreek Hill, WR (5%)** — Boone's "upside stash" flag; check where he's landed and take a flier in deep leagues.
- **Fernando Mendoza, QB, LV (7%)** — the No. 1 overall rookie QB is "keeping the seat warm" news even while Cousins is 3-0. A one-percent stash for superflex patience.
- **Zach Charbonnet (51%, PUP) & Jonah Coleman (43%, IR)** — IR-stash tier. Coleman was the week's great betrayal, but Fitz keeps him as a stash: Denver's run game has been ineffective and Payton may want the rookie's prominence in mid-October. Don't cut in a panic, don't re-add if dropped.
- **Kaleb Johnson, RB, GB (6%)** — out-snapped MarShawn Lloyd (and Brooks) in Week 3. The Packer who isn't a drop.
- **Adonai Mitchell, WR, NYJ (39%)** — finger injury dating to Week 2; Yahoo's Week 4 injury report has him on it again, so treat his return as game-time management. Hold, don't bid: his absence is what *inflated* Sadiq's ceiling.
- **Keaton Mitchell, RB, LAC (34%)** — 8-52-0 plus a jet-sweep TD; McDaniel drafted him to be an Achane-lite. Three-man committee, speculative.

## Drop candidates

- **Justin Herbert** (QB) — 14.3, 9.9, 15.0 fantasy points through three weeks, then a schedule of Seahawks-Broncos-Chiefs-bye-Rams-Texans-Ravens. Jahnke and Boone both say cut; better QBs are free.
- **Baker Mayfield** (QB) — dislocated thumb, "at least three weeks" per Bowles, rookie UDFA Jalon Daniels starting. Drop in most formats.
- **Marvin Harrison Jr.** (WR) — promoted from "regret risk" to fully droppable: five targets on 52 Brissett throws, and Michael Wilson (11-89-1) is Arizona's clear No. 1.
- **Brian Thomas Jr.** (WR) — snap shares of 35/54/39% and 1-8-0 Sunday. "Au revoir," per Fitz.
- **MarShawn Lloyd** (RB) — 15 snaps, a costly fumble, and beaten on the depth chart by Kaleb Johnson and Chris Brooks behind an awful O-line.
- **Kyle Pitts** (TE) — 1-5-0 on two targets even with Penix back and London erupting. Dead battery.
- **Chris Godwin** (WR) — 10-111-0 with a healthy Mayfield; with Daniels in, drop-with-regret at best. Same bucket for **Xavier Worthy** (10-63-1 on 15 targets, then bye).
- Also on Boone's drop list: **Malik Willis** (QB), **Tyjae Spears** and **Jonathon Brooks** (RB, IR), **Quentin Johnston**, **Jayden Reed**, **Khalil Shakir** (WR), **Hunter Henry** (TE).
