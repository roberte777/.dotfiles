Week 6 didn't just bruise the wire — it amputated from it. **Jalen Hurts** is
out for the season: a superficial calf clot progressed to a DVT with bilateral
pulmonary emboli, and blood thinners bench him for months (Schefter; the
Eagles/agent joint statement calls the long-term prognosis "very good").
**Tanner McKee** starts with 5th-round rookie Cole Payton backing him — Andy
Dalton was released. **Terry McLaurin** tore his patellar tendon Monday night
vs the 49ers (Rapoport confirmed post-MRI; Quinn called it season-ending),
**Davante Adams** avulsed the semitendinosus tendon in his knee (surgery this
week, 6–10 weeks per McVay, "optimistic" per Stotts — effectively the season in
fantasy), and **Mike Evans** left MNF with Achilles soreness that San
Francisco is treating as a multi-week deal through their Week 8 bye. The
secondary wave: **Ladd McConkey** (concussion, eight snaps — bye-week protocol,
Week 8 target), **Romeo Doubs** (shoulder AC sprain, IR for four games),
**Rashod Bateman** (shoulder), **Ashton Jeanty** (not 100% — 2.2 YPC and a
snap-share decline despite Kubiak's assurances), **Quinshon Judkins** (ankle,
missed Week 6), **Alvin Kamara** (back), **Jeremiyah Love** (ankle, possible
Week 7 return), **Cam Skattebo** (six snaps, zero touches — fumble jail after
his fourth ball-security of the year, and the Giants cut **Najee Harris**
Tuesday), **Tyler Warren** (groin), **Dalton Kincaid** (ribs), **Darnell
Mooney** (neck) and **Treylon Burks** (walked off late Monday, limited
Wednesday at best). **Lamar Jackson** (ankle) is "likely" back this week;
**Baker Mayfield** (thumb) is expected to practice Wednesday; **Caleb
Williams** is expected to start Sunday. **Jordan Mason** was designated to
return for Minnesota. Four teams idle: Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and
Washington. The consensus is unusually thick because the story is: a
quarterback vacancy in Philadelphia, a receiver vacancy in Washington, a
target vacuum in LA — and three rookies who just went off.

## Stashes & lottery tickets

- **Terrance Ferguson, TE, LAR (12–13%)** — Boone's No. 6, Fitz's top Rams TE ($6 true/$12 desperate). On IR until Week 8, but he's scored a TD in all four full games played with either Nacua or Adams off the field, including 6-54-1 on nine targets. With Adams gone for months, Ferguson is Stafford's de facto No. 2 until further notice. Bid now, cash out when the price doubles.
- **Antonio Williams, WR, WSH (6%)** — Boone's No. 4 ($5), CBS's "worth adding in all leagues." McLaurin's season is over and Williams led all Washington receivers in snaps and routes Monday night (3-50) before the bye. Use an IR slot: he doesn't play until Week 8, and he starts opposite Diggs forever after.
- **Chig Okonkwo, TE, WSH (4–16%)** — NFL.com's No. 3 target, Boone's No. 9 ($3). 60 yards on six targets and 69 on seven the last two weeks; the McLaurin vacancy is a target redistribution nobody has repriced. Same bye caveat — stash him properly.
- **Jonah Coleman, RB, DEN (28–31%)** — PFF flags him with Mason as the top IR-return RB, Fitz expects him to suit up Sunday in Arizona ($4 true/$8 desperate). Dobbins (3.3 YPC) and Harvey (3.9) are dragging a 196-yard offense, and Coleman's Week 2 flash was the Broncos' season-high 392 yards. He's a no-risk IR-slot claim if he's walked.
- **Jordan Mason, RB, MIN** — NFL.com's No. 7 priority, PFF's top short/long-term RB available. Designated to return, practices Wednesday; Aaron Jones (age-32, 482 scrimmage yards) means Minnesota goes right back to a timeshare, but a timeshare with the league's best schedule-relative backfield beat is still a startable committee member. Activate-and-watch.
- **Jordyn Tyson, WR, NO (48–54%)** — Fitz's #2 add overall, and the Norris/Smyth pod's "would be my No. 1 if not for the number." Week 9 return after the bye is the realistic path; the 8th-overall talent in a Kellen Moore air attack is a two-buck park-it while the market is asleep.
- **Christian Kirk, WR, SF (4–5%)** — designated to return with Evans' Achilles, Deebo's hamstring and Stribling (out until late November) vacating the room. Fitz and CBS both see a meaningful short role in one of the league's most efficient passes; Falcons-at-home Week 7 is the friendly window.
- **DeMario Douglas, WR, NE (8%)** — the Patriots' most impactful receiver *with* A.J. Brown back: 7-86-1, 151 yards and three TDs over two games, and Doubs' IR stint keeps slot/three-receiver work on his plate against Bears/Dolphins/Packers. Boone and CBS both list him; keep the bid at a buck.
- **Kendrick Bourne, WR, ARI (9–19%)** — NFL.com's No. 6, Fitz's $1–2: 10-117 on 16 targets the last two weeks with MHJ gone to Houston. Denver is the ugly part of the deal now; Week 8's Dallas visit is the payoff.
- **Malik Washington, WR, MIA (32–41%)** — CBS's high-floor flex and Boone's "steady WR4" counter to Tucker's boom-bust. 36 team-high targets with double-digit points in three straight before the bye. Miami's whole passing game is 849 yards of despair, so this is volume, not upside.
- **Samaje Perine, RB, CIN (5–6%)** — Boone's flex list and Fitz's "can't completely discount" handcuff: he out-carried a struggling Chase Brown 14-13 in Week 5 at 4.9 YPC. Brown's first-down slump is the entire case.
- **The handcuff cellar** — Fitz and Boone re-list the gang: Brian Robinson Jr. (43–46%, Bijian), Kaelon Black (28%, McCaffrey), Emmett Johnson (21–23%, Walker), Tyler Allgeier (~45% with Love's ankle looming), Jaylen Wright (5–6%, Gordon holds 1B but the speed matters), Audric Estimé/Jaleel McLaughlin (Judkins/Sampson dominoes in Cleveland — McLaughlin's 61.9% snap share "turns to dust" if Judkins is back, per Fitz) and the bye-week crowd (Vidal, Rodriguez Jr., Ekeler, Ray Davis).
- **Fernando Mendoza, QB, LV (5–14%)** — Fitz's stash with the Raiders 3-0-less and the "transition to Mendoza is the whole season" framing; Boone's deep-stash list agrees. One dollar, superflex upside.
- **Travis Hunter, WR/CB, JAX (7–16%)** — the week's source split: Last Word calls him a must-add after 4-59-1 on nine snaps plus IDP stats (Brian Thomas ran zero second-half snaps), Fitz calls him Fool's Gold ("simply doesn't get enough offensive snaps"). Add for IDP-plus formats or the trade-deadline lottery; do not bid real money on a nine-snap sample.

## Drop candidates

- **Jalen Hurts** (QB) — season over; cut and, if you somehow rostered McKee's handcuff, hold only the Smith/Goedert assets that McKee's trailing scripts might feed (Fitz).
- **Terry McLaurin** (WR) — torn patellar tendon, done. Fitz's eulogy is right: even the return would be a reduced 32-year-old.
- **Davante Adams** (WR) — "droppable with a chance of regret" per Fitz, but surgery plus a 6–10 week estimate that Stotts calls optimistic means he's Week 13-and-ramp-up at best. Cut.
- **Jameis Winston** (QB) — Fitz: "no longer a viable option." Benched with 11 minutes left, two picks and four sacks; McCarthy's revival makes the tiebreak inevitable.
- **Bo Nix** (QB) — Boone + Fitz + CBS chorus: 13.6 FPPG, 174.8 yards a game, and the Cardinals (not a pass-funnel you can trust) are next.
- **Justin Herbert** (QB) — Boone + CBS again, 15.3 or fewer points in all six games, McConkey concussed and bye in Week 8. Terminal.
- **Romeo Doubs** (WR) — Fitz's flat-out drop: IR four games *behind* A.J. Brown's return.
- **Tyjae Spears** (RB) — Fitz: 23.9% snaps and two touches; Pollard is 1A on a bad team.
- **Cam Skattebo** (RB) — Fitz's "acceptable to part with": fumble jail, six snaps, zero touches, and the Giants were shopping RBs all offseason.
- **MarShawn Lloyd** (RB) and **Mark Andrews** (TE) — Boone's cuts: Jacobs back in Green Bay means Lloyd is a handcuff again, and Andrews hasn't topped 50 yards all season (23-181-1 through six).
- Also on the lists: **Wan'Dale Robinson** (Fitz — 3-22 on seven targets, the slot funnel lied), **David Montgomery** and **Keaton Mitchell** (Boone), **Chris Godwin**, **Cortland Sutton** and **Michael Pittman Jr.** (Boone — roster-spot dead weight), **Jake Ferguson**/**Dalton Schultz** (Boone, the two-TE nothing), and Fool's Gold **Rashid Shaheed** (eight targets in a 51-pass Darnold fever dream) and **Tommy Tremble** (two TDs, 25-or-fewer yards in three of five). **Josh Downs** is disputed — Boone drops, Fitz holds ("better than his numbers") — middle path: hold in 14+, drop with a regret smile in shallow PPR.
