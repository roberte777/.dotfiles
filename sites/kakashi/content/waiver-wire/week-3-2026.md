Week 2 left a body count: **Caleb Williams** (hamstring, week-to-week),
**Jayden Daniels** (re-dislocated elbow, no timeline), **Saquon Barkley**
(stinger, MRI), **J.K. Dobbins** *and* **RJ Harvey** (both hamstrings), and
**David Njoku** (knee, suspected long-term). The wire is a bounty because of
it — FantasyPros graded it an "A-," which almost never happens.

## Must-adds

| Player | Pos | Team | Rost. | FAAB | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jonah Coleman** | RB | DEN | 26% | $15–25 | Both Dobbins and Harvey are hamstrung. Coleman took over (58 scrimmage yards, TD, out-snapped Badie) and Payton clearly prefers him. He likely keeps a real role even after returns. The week's consensus No. 1. |
| **Denzel Boston** | WR | CLE | 31% | $15–20 | 94–97% route share, leads the Browns in targets, touchdowns in both games. Watson is the ceiling cap, but Boston is a weekly flex today. |

| **Oronde Gadsden II** | TE | LAC | 6% | $3 | Njoku suspected out long-term and Kolar in a sling. Gadsden is a TE2 stream with TE1 weeks — he did it for a month in 2025. |

## Stashes & lottery tickets

- **Marcus Mariota, QB, WSH (0–3%)** — starts indefinitely with Daniels out; use a claim priority while he's free.
- **Tyson Bagent, QB, CHI (0%)** — if Williams' MRI says multiple weeks, he starts an offense that needs any jolt it can get.
- **Keon Coleman, WR, BUF (2%)** — 25% target share after DJ Moore's shoulder exited; day-to-day.
- **Emmett Johnson, RB, KC (27%)** — best handcuff on a contender.
- **Kaleb Johnson, RB, GB (4%)** — led Packers backs while Jacobs sits on the exempt list (court date Nov 17; don't hold your breath).
- **Johnny Mundt, TE, PHI (0%)** — Goedert (MCL) out weeks; Stowers and Calcaterra are already on IR.
- **Bryce Young, QB, CAR (33%)** — two straight 3-TD games, and Detroit's gutless pass defense is next.

## Drop candidates

- **Baker Mayfield** (QB) — ceiling of 13 points twice, now faces Flores' Vikings.
- **Kenneth Gainwell** (RB) — 33 scrimmage yards on 11 touches; depth at best.
- **Rico Dowdle** (RB) — toe injury in a shrinking committee.
- **Marvin Harrison Jr.** (WR) — one reception in two games. Drop/sell only if desperate; the market knows.
- **Jayden Reed** (WR) — stretcher injury vs the Jets (moved all extremities, thankfully). Timeline unknown.
