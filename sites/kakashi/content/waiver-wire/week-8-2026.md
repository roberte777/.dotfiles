Week 7 was, per FantasyPros' own injury report, "relative calm after the injury chaos" — so the fall-out is mostly role shifts, and they're the good kind. The headline is a return: **Jonah Coleman** came off IR and immediately led the Broncos' backfield in snaps (30 to RJ Harvey's 16 and J.K. Dobbins' 14), touches and scrimmage yards, and Sean Payton said the quiet part — "got to get him more touches. Definitely." The season-ending club is unchanged (Hurts shut down for the year, McLaurin's patellar tendon, Adams' hamstring ~two months) but its beneficiaries are live: **Tanner McKee** under center in Philadelphia scored 14 points on 222 yards, **Antonio Williams** and **Chig Okonkwo** own the Washington target vacuum, and the Rams' tight-end room runs without Ferguson (eligible to return this week). The new casualty list is bye-adjacent: Jaylin Noel (turf toe, HOU bye), Cam Skattebo and Tyrone Tracy (both hammy, NYG bye — Tracy was rostered *because* of the Skattebo fumble jail and now can't play for two weeks), Jaylen Waddle (chest, left Q4), Jahmyr Gibbs (ankle), Aaron Jones Sr. (knee), Rashid Shaheed (knee), Erick All Jr. (knee), Velus Jones Jr. and C.J. Donaldson (hammy/shoulder). Watch-list returns: **Breece Hall** is questionable for Sunday's Raiders game (quad, four straight misses; Braelon Allen's 24-touch, 134-yard outings make the landing complicated), **Zach Charbonnet** is in the final recovery stage, **Tyjae Spears** was designated to return, and **Lamar Jackson** is working the last stage while Tyler Huntley keeps starting. **Jeremiyah Love** was ruled out again — third straight game — leaving Allgeier/Knight crumbs. Two trade clocks are ticking on the wire: Jonathan Taylor-to-elsewhere (Colts 2-5, Nov 10 deadline — hence Seth McGowan) and Brian Thomas Jr. (JAG, hence Travis Hunter). Four teams idle: Houston, New Orleans, the Giants, the 49ers.

## Stashes & lottery tickets

- **Mike Washington Jr., RB, LV (30%)** — Boone's top "one-injury-away" stash and Fitz's $2/$4: 4.9 YPC against Jeanty's league-worst 3.1 (2.3 over three straight sub-30-yard games), with 2.74 yards after contact per attempt. Jeanty's receiving gravity keeps him 1A, but the early-down carries are migrating. The Jets road game could be the handoff point.
- **Jakobi Meyers, WR, JAX (45%)** — Fitz's $2/$4, RotoBaller's No. 12. 5-83 on a season-high 10 targets before the bye; 20-285-2 on the year, 10.2 yards per target. Colts run man coverage at the sixth-highest rate and BTJ is trade-bait — Meyers absorbs the vacated targets either way. The proven floor to Hunter's lottery ticket.
- **Ja'Kobi Lane, WR, BAL (17%)** — NFL.com's No. 14, Fitz's $2/$4, the FantasyPros pod's named third priority. 7-65 on 10 targets over two games back from the wrist, 28 routes and the second-most targets on the team behind Flowers. Not a starter until Lamar is *in* — but the final stage of the ankle rehab is happening now, and at 17% this is a two-buck pre-position.
- **Mason Taylor, TE, NYJ (~1%)** — USA Today's $2 dart: 5-62-2 (plus a 2-pointer) on six targets, 12 catches over three games, led Jets TEs in snaps and routes while first-round Sadiq played a limited role. Fitz's Fool's Gold label is the honest asterisk — worth rostering in 2TE/deep PPR, not worth bidding on a two-TD day. Boone notes back-to-back six-target games, too.
- **Travis Hunter, WR/CB, JAX (8–9%)** — Boone's $9: nine offensive snaps, four catches, 59 yards, a TD, and the first-read look on half of them, plus the IDP haul. With BTJ getting fewer snaps and heading toward a trade, "Hunter's offensive involvement could rise" (Boone). RotoBaller ranks him 43rd; Fitz still calls the snap count the whole story. Buy the ceiling cheap, don't start him in a must-win.
- **Brian Thomas Jr., WR, JAX (48%)** — Boone's pure trade-deadline stash: the former No. 3 overall pick put up 87-1,282-10 in 2024 and has vanished in Jacksonville. At 48% he's on the edge of available; if he lands on a receiver-needy winner of a team, you just owned his breakout for zero.
- **J.J. McCarthy, QB, NYG (11%)** — Boone's bye-week stash and Fitz's named QB stash: 20-28, 252 yards in a road upset at Houston in his first Giants start, after nearly willing the Saints game from the bench. The revival "is starting to look like the real thing" (Fitz) — buy it this week while the Giants are idle and nobody's bidding.
- **Xavier Hutchinson, WR, HOU (1%)** — USA Today's bye-week shot: the *Texans'* leader in targets (6) and receptions (3) plus a TD against the Giants — ahead of Collins, MHJ, Noel and Dell. Houston can't run it and has a wealth of passing options; one dollar parks a lottery winner for the playoff stretch.
- **Samaje Perine, RB, CIN (12%)** — USA Today's $7, RotoBaller's No. 18: four touches and a goal-line TD in the Ravens win, his second straight scoring game, and he's getting the red-zone work Chase Brown isn't. Fitz says "no pretense of stand-alone value" — but Brown's first-down slump makes this a cheap and *actual* handcuff plus flex TD hedge.
- **Tyrone Tracy Jr., RB, NYG (28%)** — Boone's bye-week stash, now with a hamstring tag. The Giants' backfield is still the chaos pit (Skattebo hammy, Singletary gone, Najee a free agent — RotoBaller wonders if anyone with weekly touches will sign him), and Tracy cleared 1,000 scrimmage yards the last two seasons. IR slot or bench park; he doesn't play until Week 9.
- **Rachaad White, RB, WSH (55%) and Rico Dowdle, RB, PIT (57–65%)** — both over the line but on the must-check wire anyway: White leads the NFL.com/FanDuel boards (No. 1 on RotoBaller's) with Croskey-Merritt on IR and a 15.7-PPR game before the bye; Dowdle caught six passes for 45 in Paris and only a Jaylen Warren injury makes him flex-forever. Bidding over $10 on either is panic money.
- **Zach Charbonnet, RB, SEA (69% on RotoBaller, more available on ESPN)** — FantasyPros and RotoBaller both put him in the final stage of ACL recovery with limited practice work resumed. Activate-and-see; Kenneth Walker and Jadarian Price (chest) make the room interesting the day he's back.
- **Tyreek Hill, WR, and Chris Bell, WR, MIA (17%/2%)** — Boone's upside-stash list and Fitz's named WR stash: Bell was third among Dolphins receivers in snaps and routes in Week 7 at 6-2/228. Miami's volume can't feed three WRs, but Douglas/Willis hype week means Hill-to-Miami trade chatter and Bell claims will spike, not dip.

## Drop candidates

- **J.K. Dobbins** (RB) — Fitz's "droppable with a chance of regret": 23% of the snaps, six carries, and the Coleman takeover is the story of the week. The TD deodorant won't keep coming.
- **Jaylen Waddle** (WR) — Fitz finally blinks: 4-48 on seven targets in a soft matchup, his *best* game in three weeks, a chest injury in Q4, and under 50 yards in every game except the Week 2 Jaguars blowup. "Not clicking with Nix" is a sentence you don't ignore for six weeks. Regret chance acknowledged; bye-crowded weeks like this are when you take it.
- **Tyler Allgeier** (RB) — Boone's cut with Fitz's agreement: two weeks of Love out and he's averaged 3.2 YPC while Knight out-snapped and out-produced him. One-injury-away upside died on the field.
- **MarShawn Lloyd** (RB) — Boone *and* Fitz: Josh Jacobs is back from the six-game suspension and Green Bay's ground game isn't good enough to support two. Handcuff-only if you own Jacobs.
- **Kendre Miller** (RB) — Boone's cut (yes, the one he recommended two weeks ago): the Saints are on bye, Etienne's hamstring path back isn't, and a 3.1 YPC zero-receiving committee back who was "the early-down lead" only by injury default doesn't need a roster spot.
- **Aaron Rodgers** and **Justin Herbert** (QB) — Boone's chorus from last week, now terminal on both sides: Rodgers' stream run met its bye and a defense with real corners; Herbert's at 48%-rostered dead weight with a Chargers bye and zero games over 15.3 points.
- **Xavier Worthy**, **Chris Godwin**, **Wan'Dale Robinson** (WR) — Boone's cuts; PFF's headline this morning says Godwin is "rapidly losing his fantasy value" outright. None of them have a route-share argument left.
- **Juwan Johnson** (TE) and **Mark Andrews** (TE) — Boone's TE pair: Johnson went 4-16, 0-0, 1-7 the last three weeks and Tyson's return erases whatever role is left; Andrews hasn't topped 50 yards all season and hasn't hit 30 in four straight. A one-TD-or-nothing TE is a drop in any week but a touchdown week.
- **Josh Downs** (WR) — the disputed one: Boone drops (no 50-yard game since Week 3, low-ceiling slot flex), Fitz explicitly holds (led Colts WRs in snaps and routes in Week 7, and IND's next five are JAX–DAL–MIA–HOU–NYG funnels). Middle path: hold in 14-team PPR, drop-with-a-better-add in shallow. Same logic spares **Jordan Addison** (lay an egg at Detroit and you have written permission) and **TreVeyon Henderson** (3.9 YPC, behind Stevenson and Kiner — but remember what he did when Stevenson was last hurt: RB3 over that span).
