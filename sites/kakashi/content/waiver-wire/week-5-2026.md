Week 4 dumped another body-pile on the wire, and the two ugliest
scenes create this week's gold. In Philadelphia, **Saquon Barkley**
left with a hamstring injury (week-to-week per Rapoport, almost
certainly out in London) and his backup **Tank Bigsby** needs core
muscle surgery and hits IR for a month-plus — leaving 1%-rostered
**Will Shipley** as the last man standing on a falling-apart roster
(CBS counts six of eleven Eagles starters expected out, including
DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert). In Cincinnati, **Ja'Marr Chase**
(concussion protocol) and **Tee Higgins** (adductor, doubtful-side)
both left Sunday, making the Bengals' No. 3 receiver and their
tight end two of the cheapest starts on the board. Also in the
win column: **Lamar Jackson** (ankle sprain, "outside chance" for
Week 5, Tyler Huntley would start), **DJ Moore**'s shoulder (again —
Keon Coleman's 6-116-1 day), **Ladd McConkey**'s foot (week-to-week),
**Jeremiyah Love**'s other ankle, **Travis Etienne** on IR, and the
Chiefs losing **Rashee Rice** (hamstring, multi-week) plus
**Tyquan Thornton** (dislocated ankle, 12-16 weeks) — which is why
the **Tyreek Hill** market is "heating up" per his agent. Seattle
put **Jadarian Price** (chest/rib) on IR and signed **Joe Mixon** to
the practice squad, clearing the runway for Emanuel Wilson.
FantasyPros grades the wire a C+ with only KC and CAR on bye.

## Stashes & lottery tickets

- **Tyreek Hill, WR, FA (23%)** — Rosenhaus says a signing is coming "soon" and the Chiefs (Rice out weeks, Thornton done for the year, on bye this week) are the obvious landing spot. Boone: not prime Tyreek, ever again — but a healthy Hill in an Andy Reid offense is WR3 upside. Bid a buck in dynasty, nothing in redraft unless it's free.
- **Joe Mixon, RB, SEA (3%)** — practice-squad signing Monday after a two-year foot-fueled hiatus. Hasn't played since 1,016 yards in 2024. Deepest-league nostalgia tax only; a $1 max if he somehow makes the active.
- **Austin Ekeler, RB, WAS (4%)** — signed off the street after Rachaad White's shoulder, played 17 snaps and went 4-29-1-0 in London. If Washington prefers the known piece over Jacory Croskey-Merritt, he has early-down value. Speculative.
- **Tank Bigsby, RB, PHI (28%)** — counterintuitive buy-low: surgery now, but he's still the 36-carry early-down piece when back if Barkley's hamstrings keep biting. Two of four games ended with Barkley injured. Cheap in points-per-week formats.
- **Kaelon Black (32%), Brian Robinson Jr. (36%), Mike Washington Jr. (39%), Emmett Johnson (26%)** — the one-injury-away backfield: McCaffrey, Bijan, Jeanty, Walker. Boone's stash board and Fitz's handcuff tiers all four. BR Jr. just scored three Monday night; hold if you own him, micro-bid if you don't.
- **Samaje Perine, RB, CIN (10-12%)** — clear No. 2 behind Chase Brown; the cheap insurance play if you spent your FAAB on Shipley.
- **Roman Wilson, WR, PIT (5%)** — TD in back-to-back weeks, 6-134-2, Metcalf's preferred flank; but only 22 of 71 snaps Sunday. Dart, not a starter bet.
- **Pat Freiermuth (15%) / Michael Mayer (8%) / Brenton Strange (33%)** — the TE streaming cellar: Strange lit up the same Bengals defense for 7-95, Mayer drew 10 targets with Bowers beside him, Henry gets a LV defense yielding 5 TE TDs. Pick per matchup.
- **Cyrus Allen (1-2%) & Jalen Royals (0%), WR, KC** — the post-Thornton depth-chart void behind the bye. Heavy opportunity cost this week, but if Hill signs they're dead; if he doesn't, Allen camped top of the rookie WR board.
- **Antonio Williams, WR, WAS (9%)** — Fitz's conditional dart: only in play if Jayden Daniels is confirmed back Sunday (McLaurin hamstring, Mariota MCL — the pass volume should come).
- **Jordyn Tyson, WR, DEN (51%)** — Schefter floated Week 7, realistically Week 9 after the bye. Add now at 51% and park him; Boone says IR-stash if available.

## Drop candidates

- **Justin Herbert** (QB) — the Week 4 call stands and hardens: no 15-point game, ≤226 yards in every loss, and Denver-KC-bye coming with McConkey (foot), Alt (neck) and Slater (ankle) all hurt. Boone, CBS and Jahnke all say cut.
- **Baker Mayfield** (QB) — thumb, at least three weeks; Jalon Daniels threw two picks and took three sacks in the first start. Drop until the timeline changes.
- **Kyler Murray** (QB) — Boone + CBS both: 17 rushing yards is the season high, 11.9 fantasy PPG recently, and a Saints defense chasing a streaming window. Even a Jefferson return may not save the stock.
- **Dalton Schultz** (TE) — Boone: with Nico Collins healthy, "the PPR scam is over." 12-140 was the Chase/Higgins injury game, not a role.
- **Jake Ferguson** (TE) — Boone's other TE cut, sitting on the drop list with Schultz.
- **Jadarian Price** (RB) — IR with chest/rib, a month gone, and even back he faces Charbonnet plus whoever Seattle keeps from this Wilson fever.
- **MarShawn Lloyd** (RB) — Boone and Fitz again: beaten by Kaleb Johnson for high-leverage snaps and carrying a fumble tax; Johnson himself is "any port in a storm" per Fitz (6%, 17 snaps, one 31-yard run, zero targets).
- **Darren Waller** (TE) — our own Week 4 must-add, formally downgraded: Fitz lists him droppable with CAR on bye and Coker expected back Week 6. If you grabbed the Lions-TE-festival logic, that festival was last Sunday.
- **Kenyon Sadiq** (TE, 56%) — 0-0-0 on four targets vs Chicago. Keep only with a 2-TE+ roster; don't pay to keep him.
- **Courtland Sutton** (WR) — Boone says drop, Fitz says hold one more week (Bryant's ankle could re-open the vacated snaps). Disagree loudly in your league chat before acting.
- Also on the drop lists: **Chris Godwin** and **Michael Pittman Jr.** (WR), **Rashod Bateman** (WR), **Rico Dowdle** and **Rachaad White** (RB, White droppable-with-a-chance-of-regret in shallow formats), **Malik Willis** (QB, unless you're desperate for the CIN game script).
